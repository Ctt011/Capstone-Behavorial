Machine Learning

Random Forest classifier detects physical movement. If you're exercising, we skip stress scoring — elevated HR during a run isn't stress.

4 features · 93.6% LOSO accuracy · CoreML export
02

Sleep Modulation

Rule-Based

Last night's sleep quality adjusts your stress sensitivity threshold. Poor sleep means your body is more vulnerable — we account for that.

Duration + quality → threshold shift
03

Stress Scoring

Signal Processing

DC/AC from PRSA, SDNN, and RMSSD computed from RR intervals, compared against your personal baseline. Not a population average — yours.

Bauer 2006 PRSA · 0–100 score · Body Battery
Output

Body Battery

Your stress score drains the battery throughout the day. Exercise drains it too — but isn't counted as stress. Recovery and calm periods stabilize it.

Honest Science

What we found — and what we didn't.

DC/AC values were not statistically significant on the WISE dataset for single-snapshot REST vs. STRESS classification (p > 0.05). This is consistent with published findings — Bahameish et al. achieved only F1=56% for Stress vs. Neutral. This is exactly why we use personal baselines and a hybrid approach instead of one-shot classification.

Training Data

WISE Dataset
