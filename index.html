Project Highlights

Innovation and Uniqueness
🧠

Problem

We built a hybrid stress detection system that combines machine learning, physiological formulas, and adaptive rules to estimate real-time stress from Apple Watch data. Instead of relying on pure ML (which struggles with small datasets), our system filters physical activity, adjusts for sleep quality, and computes a personalized stress score using heart rate variability metrics. The result is a dynamic, user-specific stress model that improves over time.

📊

Approach

Our core innovation is the Body Battery — an intuitive energy meter that translates complex physiological signals into a simple percentage (0–100%). Rather than telling users “your HRV is low,” we show them how much energy they have left. Stress drains the battery over time, while recovery activities recharge it. This makes stress visible, measurable, and actionable.

✅

Impact

Unlike typical stress apps that just say “You’re stressed,” our system closes the loop: Detect → Quantify → Drain Energy → Recommend Recovery → Measure Improvement → Adapt Baseline The battery drains based on both stress intensity and duration, making it more realistic. A long, moderate stress period impacts you more than a short spike. The system also adapts to each user’s normal HRV patterns, reducing false alerts.
